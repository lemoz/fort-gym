With thinking off, Qwen3.5 9B selected 32 waits and advanced 32,000 ticks. With thinking on, it attempted construction and designated two trees for chopping across 18 commands and 36,000 ticks. Neither attempt completed a workshop, placed bed or farm. Both ended with seven dwarves and 53 measured drink units.

The thinking trial paused when its nineteenth response used all 4,096 output tokens without returning an action. Its checkpoint covers all 18 commands and 19 responses, totaling 184,117 tokens. The baseline stopped at its one-segment limit with 321,472 tokens. Both have complete checkpoints and verified cleanup. Model API charges were $0; operating costs are unknown.

These runs used the same model, original save and settings except for thinking. A shared adapter limit clipped thinking requests from 2,500 to 2,000 ticks; the counts above are actual time. One pair with different stopping points is not a ranking, and neither fortress reached year two.
