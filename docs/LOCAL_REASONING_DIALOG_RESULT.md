## What stopped play

Step 82 advanced 2,339 of 2,500 requested ticks before a liaison meeting opened.
The native controller paused cleanly on `viewscreen_textviewerst`, at year 30,
tick 220,140. The next model decision was WAIT with 2,500 ticks, not INTERACT.
The native clock refused that blocking-dialog baseline with
`interrupt_baseline_invalid`. It advanced zero ticks and stayed paused.
The campaign loop escalated this to a terminal ValueError. The model's invalid
choice and the harness's fatal handling are separate facts. This is not evidence
of fortress collapse or a provider/output-token failure.

The public failure category remains unclassified because the frozen producer did
not emit a typed terminal code. The audit preserves the specific cause above
without inventing a code in historical runtime records.

## Recovery and teardown

All ten checkpoints at eight-decision intervals through 80 verified their native
save inventory, bound files, usage and parent lineage. The final saved checkpoint
predates three committed decisions and four returned model decisions. No complete
terminal save was obtained while the dialog was open. Checkpoint 80 must not be
presented as a lossless resume of the end state, or have the later usage discarded.

The original owner exited, copied evidence, stopped its container, and shut down
the local VM. Its model and tunnel processes were independently absent, the
listener was free, and the Docker context was unchanged. No restart occurred.
