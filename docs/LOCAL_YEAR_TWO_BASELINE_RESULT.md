# Local year-two baseline: complete first segment, no development

The September 6 direct-response Qwen3.5 9B campaign completed its declared first
segment. All 32 model-selected commands were accepted WAITs, advancing 32,000
native ticks from year 30, tick 16801 to tick 48801. This is about 0.079 elapsed
years, not a year-two result. There were no human gameplay actions or forced waits.

Seven citizens remained. Native drink stock changed from 60 to 53 units. No room,
workshop, placed bed or farm was completed. Food, wood and stone stock values lack
the required source/freshness evidence and remain unknown in this projection.
Stock changes do not establish production or consumption rates.

All 32 responses returned normally, with 80 to 153 completion tokens each, and
321,472 total reported tokens. The current calendar, population, work, crew and
fort facts matched the retained observations in every actual packed request.
Repeated waiting was not an output-limit pause. Its cause is not established.

Independent verification passed for periodic checkpoints at decisions 8, 16 and
24 and the final checkpoint at 32. The final save, trace and usage journal cover
all decisions and returned usage. The controller stopped at its one-segment
invocation limit with a verified continuation checkpoint, not fortress collapse.
The game container, owned model process, tunnel and local VM were verified stopped.

Metered model API charges were **$0**, with no hosted-provider calls or cloud VM.
Hardware, electricity and application costs remain unmeasured; this is not a
reconciled total project cost.
